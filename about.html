OUR PURPOSE

Student support shouldn't feel scattered.

Pathway AI brings important student-support tools together into one university-connected experience.

Instead of navigating separate systems for financial wellness, resources, opportunities, and guidance, students can use Pathway AI as a central starting point for finding support and deciding what to do next.

The goal is to provide students with a clear, supportive experience while giving Chicago State University the ability to manage the resources and opportunities students see.
